<IntroParagraph>I recently got the chance to give a talk at [Granola](https://www.granola.ai/)'s [Design Engineering night](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PL4tqAmtnUMjO4EmmwJ-D2edgLardYJrvv) about speculative design and practical tools for developing more interesting theories of the future. I think design engineers, in particular, are the perfect people to imagine and prototype novel software ideas.</IntroParagraph>

At GitHub Next, our team's job is to explore possible futures for software development by building prototypes. I share a bit about how we do that, followed by some practical techniques for trying it yourself: collecting weak signals, building theories about where things are heading, and making artefacts that let other people poke at those theories too.

<div data-slide="69" data-source-slide="html/59-play-with-your-ideas-copy-3.html" data-fragment="-1">

{/* 51 · Play with your ideas copy 3 */}

Cricket-powder cereal: an artefact from the future.

Not enough animal protein for everyone — what if we ate insects? A big question a few years ago.

Insects are high in protein; climate change could limit how many animals we can keep.

Speculative design makes that future familiar and domestic: breakfast cereal.


</div>

<div data-slide="70" data-source-slide="html/60-play-with-your-ideas-copy.html" data-fragment="-1">

{/* 51 · Play with your ideas copy */}

Stay-at-home mums using OpenClaw for homeschooling and household organisation.

Historical parallel: early 20th-century women, vacuum cleaners, microwaves, washing machines. Less manual labour; more able to enter the workforce.

Debated, but I have the stats: this did happen.

My version: one kid, a house to renovate.

ChatGPT handles cognitive labour: next-size-up clothes, weekly meals, contractors, tracking home and family tasks.


</div>

{/* 51 · Play with your ideas copy 4 */}

Throwback adverts about that trend: love the retro look and the promise of agents.

A historical parallel with women, feminism and labour — not the trad wife dream. Some people on Twitter missed that.

Agents could relieve domestic cognitive labour, which falls more heavily on women.

The contrast: built mainly by single 20-year-olds in San Francisco with no domestic life.
