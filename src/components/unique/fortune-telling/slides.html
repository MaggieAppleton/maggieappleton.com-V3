We’re in a weird time. Everyone’s got whiplash from the speed of progress.

Hard to understand today’s reality, let alone figure out how to react.

Why even attempt to predict the future? (clearly a fool’s errand)

Everyone needs a speculative designer scout mindset right now.

We can’t just give up or opt out because things are moving too fast. We need to think like fortune tellers and attempt to crystal ball.

Design engineers are the perfect people to figure out the shape of the future.

Uniquely positioned to prototype theories of the future in convincing ways.

Agents can “draw the rest of the owl” enough for you to make proof-of-concepts without full-stack skills (although they help).

Play with your ideas

Doing (cheesy) structured exercises to think deeply about your theories of the future and understand your assumptions.

04Play with your ideas
Invert itList what you’re assuming, then invert each one.
We prompt the agents
→ the agents prompt us
We build the software with agents
→ the software builds itself
Split itBreak the idea down into parts and list the attributes of the idea. Change one part at a time
OpenClaw: sandbox + memory + permissions + initiative.

Can the agent share memories within a team or family?
Can the agent tactfully forget sensitive facts or details?
Could an agent choose to delete evidence to protect its human?
04Play with your ideas
Maximise and minimisePlay out extreme versions of the future, in both directions.
Max: Running a thousand agents a day costs £1. What’s possible? Min: Agent intelligence becomes 10x more expensive. We have to ration. How do we adapt?
BrutethinkPick something unrelated and force it to match your idea.
Seasonal harvest: agent work happens in seasons. What kind of work maps to which season? Does your agent only pick what’s ripe?Postal service: how do agents mail each other? Who is the Royal Mail for agents?
